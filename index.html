What sensible means in practice

It has to pay for itself.

AI earns its place by moving a number that matters: revenue, cost, risk or time. If it can't, it waits.

Production, or it didn't happen.

A model in a notebook helps no one. The real work is getting it running, monitored and owned by your team.

Guardrails are part of the product.

Unintended consequences get designed out at the start, not patched after launch.

Know when to stop.

Some AI ideas should end. Spotting them early saves more than any model will.
